## FD2 Community

Open source software projects such as FarmData2 rely on virtual communities of volunteers, interns, students and faculty to perform the development, testing and maintenance of the software. Like other communities these open source communities have norms and regulations. Review the following documents in your fork of the FarmData2-School repository so that you are familiar with the norms and regulations of the FarmData2 community:

- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` - A set of guidelines outlining appropriate behavior and communication within the FarmData2 community.
- `LICENSE.md` - Information about the copyright and licensing of the intellectual property contributions made to the FarmData2 project.
  - Be sure to also review the "Developer Certificate of Origin" that is linked in in the "Contributions" section of the `LICENSE.md`.

## Restart your FarmData2 Development Environment

If you need a refresher on how to restart your FarmData2 Development Environment:

1. Find the installation directions in your FarmData2-School repository (hint: See `INSTALL.md`).
2. Go to the instructions for "Running in GitHub Codespaces".
3. Follow the instructions in the section on "Restarting a FarmData2 Development Environment in Codespaces."
