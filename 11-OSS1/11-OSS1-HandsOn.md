# 11 - OSS1 - In Class Hands-On 

In this hands-on exercise you will take on the role of a **contributor** who is helping the project by reviewing and enhancing bug reports.

## Preliminaries

You have each been assigned one of the bug reports written for the Tutorial by one of your peers. 

1. Open the issue tracker for the FarmData2-School upstream where you created your issue during the Tutorials.
2. Click the "Assignees" drop down and enter or choose your GitHub username.
3. Open the issue that has been assigned to you.

## Replicating the Issue

Being able to reproduce an issue is an important first step in being able to solve it. Thus, reviewing issues to ensure they can be replicated can be a valuable contribution to a project.

1. Imagine a general farmOS/FarmData2 user who is familiar with how to use the application but does not know anything about this bug prior to reading this bug report.
2. Working from the perspective of that user, attempt to follow the "Steps to Reproduce" the issue as they currently appear in the bug report.
3. Consider whether statement a or b below is more accurate:  
   a) You have no doubt that the imagined user would be able to follow the steps with no difficulty and see the "Observed behavior" exactly as reported.  
   b) There is a reasonable possibility that the imagined user would benefit from a revision of the steps or the "Observed Behavior". 
4. If you answered:  
   a) React to the body of the issue with a 👍.  
   b) Add a comment to the issue that improves the "Steps to Reproduce" and/or the "Observed Behavior."
      - This comment should not not be written as criticism of the original issue or its author. - It should be written in a constructive, helpful tone and targeted at a reader who had difficulty following the "Steps to Reproduce".

## Adding Information to the Issue

As contributors and maintainers review issues, attempt to replicate them, and work on fixing them, they will also often add additional information about the issue to the bug report.

1. Re-read the full bug report with the benefit of the class discussion of the issue in mind.
2. Consider whether statement a or b below is more accurate:  
   - a) The bug report is complete and clearly captures all aspects of the bug.  
   - b) The bug report is missing one or more aspects of the bug. 
3. If you answered:  
   - a) React to the body of the issue with a ❤️.  
   - b) Add a comment to the issue that adds the missing information.
     - Your comment should: 
       - describe the additional aspect(s) of the issue.
       - give "Steps to reproduce" that can be followed to observe the additional aspect(s) of the issue.
       - describe the "Observed behavior" that is expected when the the "Steps to reproduce" are followed.
     - As before, this comment should not not be written as criticism of the original issue or its author. 
     - It should be written in a constructive, helpful tone and targeted at a reader who had difficulty following the "Steps to Reproduce".
